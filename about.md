## About Me

I'm **Heng Ma**, a Research Scientist at **Argonne National Laboratory**.

My research sits at the intersection of **machine learning**, **physical chemistry**, and **biophysics** — building computational tools and methods that accelerate scientific discovery.

### Research Focus

- **AI-Driven Molecular Simulations** — Combining deep learning with physics-based models for protein folding, molecular dynamics, and biomolecular behavior
- **Computational Drug Discovery** — Virtual screening, structure-based drug design, and understanding viral protein interactions (SARS-CoV-2 Mpro, spike dynamics)
- **High-Performance Scientific Computing** — Scalable infrastructure for running AI and simulations at scale (100–1000× speedups)
- **Structural Biology & Biophysics** — Membrane systems, electron transfer dynamics, and protein-membrane interactions

### Key Contributions

- Pioneered AI-driven multiscale simulation approaches that reveal mechanisms of SARS-CoV-2 spike protein dynamics and respiratory aerosol transmission
- Developed genome-scale language models (GenSLMs) for understanding viral evolutionary dynamics
- Built streaming AI + HPC frameworks that enable dramatically faster biomolecular simulations
- Contributed to integrated pipelines for pandemic drug discovery combining ML and physics-based methods
